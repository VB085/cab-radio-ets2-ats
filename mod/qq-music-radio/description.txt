Cab Radio - stream YOUR local audio into your truck radio.
把本机音频开进你的驾驶室 - 电脑上放什么，游戏电台里就播什么.

The actual audio engine is the Cab Radio Companion (a small tray app for
Windows). This mod is the discovery point: once the Companion is installed,
it automatically keeps the "Cab Radio" station listed in your game radio
and serves the stream at http://127.0.0.1:17890/stream.mp3.

How to set up:
1. Install VB-CABLE (vb-audio.com)
2. Route your player's output to "CABLE Input" (players with a device
   option: pick it in settings; apps without one: Windows Settings ->
   Sound -> Volume mixer -> force-route the app)
3. Run the Companion (tray icon; optional "Run at startup")
4. Start the game and select "Cab Radio" in the radio

Everything runs locally on 127.0.0.1 - no internet connection needed.
任何能出声的程序都可以接入：播放器、浏览器皆可。全程本机运行，无需联网。
