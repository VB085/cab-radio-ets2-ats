Cab Radio - stream YOUR local audio into your truck radio.
把本机音频开进你的驾驶室 - 电脑上放什么，游戏电台里就播什么.

This mod only adds ONE radio entry pointing to a local stream on your own PC:
http://127.0.0.1:17890/stream.mp3

The sound comes from the Cab Radio Companion on your computer. Any audio app
works - route its output through VB-CABLE and the Companion does the rest.

Setup:
1. Install VB-CABLE (vb-audio.com)
2. Route your player's output to "CABLE Input" (e.g. QQ Music:
   Settings -> Audio -> output device; for apps without that option use
   Windows Settings -> Sound -> Volume mixer to force-route the app)
3. Run the Companion
Done - select "Cab Radio" in the game radio.
